# CodeAlpha Data Visualization Project

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship.

The objective of this project is to analyze a sales dataset and transform the raw data into meaningful visualizations. The analysis focuses on identifying sales patterns across different regions, sales representatives, product categories, and months.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 1000 sales records and 14 columns.

Some of the important columns include:

- Product ID
- Sale Date
- Sales Representative
- Region
- Sales Amount
- Quantity Sold
- Product Category
- Unit Cost
- Unit Price
- Customer Type
- Discount
- Payment Method
- Sales Channel

The dataset contains no missing values.

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Visualizations

### 1. Total Sales by Region

This visualization compares the total sales generated by each region.

The North region generated the highest total sales, while the South region generated the lowest.

### 2. Total Sales by Sales Representative

This visualization compares the sales performance of different sales representatives.

David generated the highest total sales, while Charlie generated the lowest.

### 3. Total Sales by Product Category

This visualization compares sales across different product categories.

Clothing generated the highest total sales, while Food generated the lowest.

### 4. Monthly Sales Trend

This visualization shows how total sales changed throughout 2023.

Sales fluctuated throughout the year, with several strong and weak months rather than a continuous upward or downward trend.

## Key Insights

- The North region was the highest-performing region with total sales of approximately $1.37 million.
- David was the highest-performing sales representative with total sales of approximately $1.14 million.
- Clothing was the highest-performing product category with total sales of approximately $1.31 million.
- Monthly sales varied considerably throughout 2023.
- The analysis helps identify high-performing regions, representatives, and product categories.
